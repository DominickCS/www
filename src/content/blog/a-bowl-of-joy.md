I bet you can see where I am going with this based on the mouth-watering pic I added at the beginning of this post.

Let me introduce to you my favorite meal, the __Teriyaki Beef Bowl__

Funnily enough, the cooking directions are just as short as the ingredients list:

<ol>
  <li>Prepare a pot of water based on your preferred rice amount, the ration of rice to ration typically is <b>one (1) cup rice to (2) cups water</b>, adjust to preference.</li>
  <li>Clean excess starches on rice, rinsing and draining your rice until the water is <b>clear.</b></li>
  <li>Bring the pot of water to boil and stir in rice. Let the rice boil for about a <b>minute uncovered.</b></li>
  <li>Cover the rice pot and simmer on a lesser heat (I use the low setting on my stove)</li>
  <li>Heat a medium saucepan on your stove on a <b>medium-high heat level</b>, and brown the ground beef.</li>
  <li>Drain the beef of excess fat using a strainer into an extra bowl.</li>
  <li>Return the beef to the saucepan to add the teriyaki sauce.</li>
  <li>Pour a liberal amount of teriyaki sauce onto the beef and mix.</li>
  <li>Add the cooked steamed rice to a bowl and top the beef on top of the bed of rice</li>
  <li>(Optional) Add your vegetables of choice to the top (I add avocado to mine for a creamier taste)</li>
</ol>

That's all the steps to craft a wonderful meal.
